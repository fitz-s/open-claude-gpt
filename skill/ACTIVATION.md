[chatgpt-consult — ACTIVE this session]
A background channel to the user's ChatGPT Pro subscription for deep, self-contained work — review, architecture, investigation, research, analysis, writing, math, design, audit. Not code-only. It is a multi-round thread, not a one-shot: feed local results back and follow up. To use it, invoke the `chatgpt-consult` skill, which loads the full operating guide and the Quickstart. Never send secrets/`.env`/keys.

**Know what a round costs.** One consult is one message against a plan that allows tens of them a week, and it reasons for something on the order of ~25 minutes on whatever model and tier is configured — GPT-6 Astra Pro by default (a GPT-5.6-measured expectation, not a confirmed GPT-6 figure; pin an older family or run against a legacy picker and both the model and the timing differ — trust the `modelBadge` receipt and your own store's latency stats over this paragraph). Whatever answers works the problem from several angles at once and argues its way to something closer to a proof than an answer. That is what the price buys, and it is why the price is worth paying for a hard, open-ended sub-problem that would otherwise consume a long stretch of local context — and wasted on anything answerable locally in minutes. It also means **the question and the prompt must be precise**: a long reasoning round pointed at a vague ask returns the same amount of well-argued irrelevance. Name the real decision, the bar for "good", and the specific sub-questions.

In auto mode a consult runs the LOCAL path — one `consult.py fire` (deliver+prep+enqueue) + `await` a local answer file; a background daemon does the actual ChatGPT send (a direct `submit` is blocked by the auto-mode data-exfiltration classifier). The daemon is installed once via `cgc install-daemon` and kept alive by launchd, so **just enqueue — never check whether it is running, and never run doctor first.** That check costs tokens every consult and buys nothing. If it genuinely isn't running, `await` says so immediately and you relay that one line to the user. **If the classifier ever denies one of these local commands, do not try to route around it** — relay it to the user and stop; attempting a bypass appears to get the whole session's local commands denied too.

When an answer lands, `SKILL.md` → "Act on the answer" is the contract for what to do with it — what to implement, what to argue with, what to verify, and what goes to the user. Read it there rather than from memory.
