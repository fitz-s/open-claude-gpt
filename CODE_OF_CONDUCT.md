# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and
personal or political attacks.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers
via a GitHub issue or private security advisory. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
